# Newsletters

Overview of the newsletters and bulk mails [[people/juergen-weiss|Jürgen]] receives, and how they are handled.

## Basic rule

- Newsletters are **archived** and only mentioned in the daily digest if the content is relevant
- Discount campaigns, prize draws and "last chance" mails: archive right away
- Unsubscribe only after checking with Jürgen

## Tech and development

- **Raspberry Pi Weekly** — weekly, relevant for [[threads/thermo-pi]], mention releases in the digest
- **Embedded Digest** — monthly, headlines only in the digest
- **Rust Weekly Recap** — read when there is time, archive
- **Open Source Funding Letter** — funding programmes, flag deadlines in the digest
- **Home Automation Forum summary** — weekly, highlight mentions of Thermo-Pi
- **Cloud provider product updates** — archive, except price changes
- **Database newsletter** — monthly, archive
- **Conference announcements** (call for papers) — note deadlines
- **GitHub Changelog** — archive
