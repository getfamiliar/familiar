# Grandma Gisela

Gisela Weiß, Jürgen's paternal grandmother; died 2024-11-18 aged 94 and left Jürgen the "Alte Mühle" (old mill house).

## Person

- Born 1930, lived in the **Alte Mühle**, Mühlweg 2, Unterbrunn, until the end
- Widowed since 1998, very independent, baked crumble cake every Sunday
- Three children: Jürgen's father Klaus (died 2011) and the daughters [[people/aunt-hedi|Hedi]] and [[people/aunt-roswitha|Roswitha]]

## Death and inheritance

- **2024-11-18**: died in the Unterbrunn care home
- Funeral 2024-11-27 at Unterbrunn cemetery
- Will: Jürgen inherits the **house** alone, the daughters Hedi and Roswitha the cash assets
- Certificate of inheritance and land registry → [[threads/inheritance-land-registry]]
- The house itself → [[threads/alte-muehle]]

## Memories

- Her recipe notebook is in Ines' kitchen
- The grave is tended by Aunt Hedi; Jürgen pays for grave care (yearly in March)
