# Phishing patterns

Collection of phishing and scam mails [[people/juergen-weiss|Jürgen]] has received, with typical warning signs.

## Fake parcel notifications

- Subject like "Your parcel could not be delivered – schedule an appointment"
- Urgent: pay a delivery fee of 1.99 € within a 24-hour deadline
- Link to a foreign domain, not the real parcel service
- **2026-05-19**, **2026-07-02**, **2026-09-11**: variants received, deleted

## Fake bank mails

- "Your account has been temporarily blocked – confirm urgently"
- "Final deadline: update your account details by 23:59 today"
- Senders imitate Nordbank, see also [[threads/nordbank-pushtan-incident]]
- **2026-08-06**: variant shortly after the real incident — particularly nasty

## Fake invoices

- "Outstanding invoice – final reminder before debt collection", attachment as ZIP
- "Your invoice no. 2026-xxxx is overdue, please pay immediately"
- **2026-06-23**, **2026-10-01**: received, attachment not opened

## Fake appointments and tasks

- Calendar invitations from strangers with "Urgent appointment: your open tasks"
- "Reminder: the deadline for your tax refund is expiring"
- **2026-10-14**: calendar spam with a link, declined and reported

## Warning signs

- Time pressure (deadline, urgent, immediately), threats (block, debt collection), unusual sender domain
- Never click links in such mails; if in doubt, check directly in the customer portal
