# Side Project Meetup (SPM)

Meetup for software developers with their own projects, **SPM** for short, organized by [[people/yusuf-demir]]; [[people/juergen-weiss|Jürgen]] takes part with [[threads/thermo-pi]].

## Format

- Meets **every second Thursday**, 7:00 pm, at the [[places/zum-schwan-inn]] (back room)
- Each round one person presents their project (10-minute demo, then questions)
- No slides, laptop on the table
- About 8–12 people, core: Yusuf, Jürgen, [[people/lisa-tran]], three or four regulars

## Projects in the group

- **Thermo-Pi** (Jürgen) — heating controller on the Raspberry Pi, see [[threads/thermo-pi]]
- **PlantWatch** (Yusuf) — moisture sensors for house plants with notifications
- **Brotzeit** (Lisa) — sourdough timer app: feeding times, proofing, baking plan
- Various smaller tinkering projects of the regulars
