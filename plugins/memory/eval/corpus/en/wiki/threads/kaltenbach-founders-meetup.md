# Kaltenbach founders' meetup

Monthly meetup for founders and business angels from the region, organized by [[people/nele-hoffmann]].

## Format

- Every last Tuesday of the month, 6:30 pm, in the coworking space above the town library
- Two or three pitches of 7 minutes each, then open networking
- [[people/juergen-weiss|Jürgen]] attends as a business angel and consultant, see [[threads/dealflow]]

## Pitches

- **2026-04-28**: [[people/kai-wendt]] pitches **Lumora** — smart glasses for warehouse order picking. Vision: "clear sight in the warehouse", workers see the next pick right in their field of view. Kai talks a lot about foresight and a clear view of the supply chain.
- **2026-04-28**: second pitch: platform for farm shops
- **2026-06-30**: pitch of a learning platform for apprentices
- **2026-09-29**: Kai Wendt presents the Lumora pre-seed round (target 600,000 €) — foresight and a strong vision, but no paying customer yet
- **2026-10-27**: pitch of a cargo-bike sharing startup
