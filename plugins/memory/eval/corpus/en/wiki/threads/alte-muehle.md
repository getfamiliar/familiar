# Alte Mühle

The inherited estate of [[people/grandma-gisela|Grandma Gisela]] at Mühlweg 2, 35796 Unterbrunn; owned by [[people/juergen-weiss|Jürgen]] since 2024.

## Status

- Owner: Jürgen (sole heir after Grandma Gisela's death on 2024-11-18), see [[threads/inheritance-land-registry]]
- Former grain mill, residential house with outbuilding, plot of about 1,900 m² by the stream
- The property is empty; heating switched off, water shut off
- **Sale paused** since 2026-01-31

## Renovation (rejected)

- End of 2024 / early 2025 briefly considered renovating the house to live in or rent out
- **2025-03**: architect's cost estimate: about **280,000 €** (roof, windows, heating, wiring, damp basement)
- **2025-04-10**: decision against the renovation — too expensive, too far away, no time. Nothing was started.

## Sale

- **2025-05**: broker [[people/ralf-szymanski]] (Szymanski Immobilien) hired, valuation about 240,000 €
- **2025-06**: house listed, asking price 259,000 €
- **2025-07 to 2025-11**: nine viewings, two interested buyers drop out after the damp survey
- **2025-12-03**: offer of 190,000 € — rejected by Jürgen
- **2026-01-31**: broker contract expired, not renewed
- No suitable buyer found; Jürgen wants to restart the sale in 2027 at the earliest

## Ongoing

- Property tax and building insurance keep running (about 1,100 €/year)
- Neighbour Mr Brandl checks on the place every two weeks and mows the lawn
- **2026-10-18**: Jürgen was there, cleared leaves from the gutter, no new damage
