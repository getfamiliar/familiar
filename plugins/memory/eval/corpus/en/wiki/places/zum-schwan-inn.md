# Gasthaus Zum Schwan

Traditional inn at Marktplatz 4 in Kaltenbach; meeting place of the [[threads/developer-meetup|Side Project Meetup]].

## Address

- **Marktplatz 4, 35791 Kaltenbach**
- Side room "Schwanenstube" for up to 20 people, Wi-Fi available

## Regulars

- Side Project Meetup every 2nd Thursday of the month from 19:00
- Asparagus dinner with the aunts in spring

## Notes

- Closed on Mondays
- Reservations by phone only, landlord Mr Bauer
- Good schnitzel, limited vegetarian menu
